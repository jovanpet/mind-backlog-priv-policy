# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2025_

## Overview

This app is designed to help you reflect, organize your thoughts, and manage problems mindfully.  
We care deeply about your privacy. This document explains what information is (and isn’t) collected, stored, and shared when you use the app.

---

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers.**

All information you enter (such as problems, notes, or reflections) is stored **locally on your device** using Apple’s secure storage systems (UserDefaults, Core Data, or similar).  
Your data stays private and never leaves your phone unless you manually back it up through iCloud or another Apple service.

---

## Analytics and Tracking

This app does **not** include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.  
We don’t use cookies, external APIs, or log personal information of any kind.

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not** share your information with any third parties.  
There are no integrations with social networks, ad platforms, or external databases.

---

## Your Control

You are in full control of your data.  
You can delete all app data at any time by removing the app from your device.  
If iCloud backup is enabled, deleting the app will remove local data, and you can manage any iCloud backup data through your Apple ID settings.

---

## Security

All data is stored securely within Apple’s app sandbox environment.  
No one other than you can access it.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published here and within the app.  
Any updates will remain consistent with the app’s privacy-first philosophy.
