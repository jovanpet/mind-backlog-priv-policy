## 💡 Common Questions

**Q: My data disappeared after reinstalling the app.**  
A: All information is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, data is removed with it. iCloud backup can restore your information if enabled.

**Q: Does the app sync between devices?**  
A: Not yet, but it’s something being explored thoughtfully for the future.

**Q: Is my information private?**  
A: Yes. All entries stay on your device and are never shared or collected.
